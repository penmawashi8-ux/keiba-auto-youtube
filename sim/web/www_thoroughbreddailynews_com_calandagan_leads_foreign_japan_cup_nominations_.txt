Calandagan Leads Foreign Japan Cup Nominations
Calandagan Leads Foreign Japan Cup Nominations
Thursday, October 15, 2026
at 3:27 pm |
Back to:
Europe
,
Shared News Europe
Updated: October 15, 2026 at 3:27 pm
Calandagan
|
Scoop Dyga
A total of 22 foreign-based horses, including defending champion
Calandagan
(
Gleneagles
), have been nominated for the G1 Japan Cup on Sunday, November 29, the Japan Racing Association (JRA) announced on Thursday.
The Aga Khan Studs runner is joined by Francis-Henri Graffard stablemate
Goliath
(Adlerflug) from France, while among the four from Britain are G1 King George heroine
Kalpana
(Study Of Man), G1 Tattersalls Gold Cup hero
Almaqam
(Lope De Vega), GI Christophe Clement Turf Stakes heroine
Survie
(Churchill) and Harry Charlton's
Wine Dark Sea
(Ulysses).
From Ireland, Aidan O'Brien sends five in G1 Irish Derby scorer
Benvenuto Cellini
(
Frankel
), multiple French Classic winner
Diamond Necklace
(
St Mark's Basilica
),
Jan Brughel
(Galileo),
Lambourn
(Australia) and
Minnie Hauk
(
Frankel
). His son Joseph is represented by G1 Irish Oaks heroine
Johanna Walsh
(
Sea The Stars
). Willie Mullins, Jessica Harrington and Dermot Weld are represented by GI Breeders' Cup Turf hero
Ethical Diamond
(Awtaad),
Hotazhell
(Too Darn Hot) and
Purview
(
Kingman
), respectively.
A trio are signed on from the U.S. in
Atitlan
(
The Factor
), the winner of the GII John Henry Turf Champion Stakes for Richard Mandella, Grade I winner
Far Bridge
(English Channel) for Miguel Clement and
Teao
(Ya Primo) for John Sadler. The Australian quartet consists of Chris Waller's Group 1-winning pair of
Aeliana
(Castelvecchio) and
Birdman
(Free Eagle), while Gai Waterhouse and Adrian Bott field multiple Group 1 scorer
Sir Delius
(
Frankel
). The field is rounded out by Matt Laurie's
Treasurethe Moment
(Alabama Express).
